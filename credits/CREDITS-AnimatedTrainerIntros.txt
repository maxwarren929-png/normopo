Animated Trainer Intros [DBK Add-On] 1.0.1
Resource: https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1667/
Author and plugin development: Lucidious89
Additional supplied metadata credit: Luka S.J.
The animation code builds on the Animated Pokemon System/EBDX work.
Trainer sprites and mugshots originate in Pokemon games and retain their original ownership.

Requires Pokemon Essentials v21.1 and Deluxe Battle Kit >=1.2.7.
This resource is separate from the essentials-cli MIT license. Original notices,
credits and author terms remain applicable. No permission for unrestricted
redistribution of Pokemon artwork is claimed.

Installation locally renames PBS/Plugins/[DBK] Animated Trainers to
PBS/Plugins/[DBK] Animated Trainer Intros so that the DBK compiler can find it
using the supplied metadata name. Plugin source files are unchanged.
Pinned archive and installed-file hashes are in plugin-sources.json.
