# Isaac's Mega Breloom

Isaac's level-55 Breloom now holds Breloomite and his trainer inventory includes a Mega Ring. It uses the normal enemy Mega mechanism and existing DBK presentation. His team remains Quagsire, Breloom, Flapple and Ceruledge. No extra Pokémon or NPC is added.

Mega Breloom is form 1, Grass/Fighting with Technician. Provisional stats in HP/Attack/Defense/Special Attack/Special Defense/Speed order are 60/160/100/60/80/100, total 560, a 100-point increase over ordinary Breloom. No new signature move or ability is introduced. Isaac retains Spore, Mach Punch, Bullet Seed and Rock Tomb.

Artwork uses the selected native IF `355.368b` Breloom + Haxorus cell, credited to **hero.drawing & ramxxxxxxxxxse**. It is resolved locally with networking blocked and the original 96×96 RGBA cell is preserved. Ordinary Breloom art is unchanged. Mirrored backs, identical shiny palettes and the copied stone icon are placeholders. The front contact sheet was inspected.

Players can roll ordinary Breloom in Hoenn to Unova Champions, equip the website's gifted Breloomite and Mega Evolve. Existing saves receive the new stone even if they completed an earlier gift. The per-item gift list prevents repeat grants. No banner pools, pull budgets or Gym levels change.

New source files are `tools/import_mega_breloom.py`, `pokemon_forms_Mega_Breloom.txt`, `pokemon_metrics_Mega_Breloom.txt`, `items_Mega_Breloom.txt`, native art and `CREDITS-Mega-Breloom.txt`. Trainer changes are in `trainers_GymTests.txt` and website gift migration is in `tools/web_mega_gift.rb`.

Release compilation validates the new form, item, metrics and Isaac's trainer record. No new real battle, browser gameplay or physical Safari verification is claimed. Stats and ability are provisional pending user balance decisions.
