## What public sources establish—and don't

The original Eevee Expo resource says the tileset was made available as a resource in the French dev-kit/fangame community and points to the Pokémon Workshop forum topic. The source project author AenaonDusky separately says users may use that version of Kanto provided they credit JohtoBlaziken and La Pampa (and La Pampa's associated credit list). Pokémon Workshop's current official credits page has an “Epic Adventures tileset” heading and publishes the contributor list above.

The original forum topic URL linked by the pack (https://pokemonworkshop.com/forum/index.php?topic=409) currently returns HTTP 404 to fetching; no primary-post license text or broader redistribution terms could be verified. The accessible pages support attribution as the stated condition for AenaonDusky's Kanto version, but do not provide a clear, independently verified license/permission from every Epic Adventures tileset contributor to redistribute the tileset in a public game or archive. Noncommercial status and crediting alone do not settle that chain. This does not establish commercial rights or permission to redistribute the assets as a standalone unrestricted tileset. The Normanhurst integration uses only selected layouts and necessary graphics from the author's advertised fangame resource, carries the complete associated credits, and retains these limits rather than relicensing the art under MIT. Broader rights remain unverified.
